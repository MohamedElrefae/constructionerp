## 2. Attempt and aggregation rules — proposed

A campaign binds exactly one reviewed catalog revision and scope. A case contains mandatory preapproved variants; one attempt executes exactly one variant in one bound disposable generation. Each attempt requires its own current authorization; a stopped attempt cannot resume, though safe cleanup can proceed. Correlation and READ ONLY proof are transaction- and attempt-specific; no proof transfers across attempts or sessions. New attempts, including after FAIL or INCOMPLETE, need fresh scoped authorization and must never use broader privilege after a stop.

Each independently reconciled attempt has one terminal result: PASS, FAIL, UNKNOWN or NOT_RUN. Expected denial yields PASS only when its exact preapproved denial class, independent positive oracle, fixture binding and all other conditions including cleanup and sealing are demonstrated; an incidental error or unqualified/absent witness is UNKNOWN, never PASS. A proven contrary outcome is FAIL; contradictory or untrusted evidence is UNKNOWN and blocks acceptance. NOT_RUN denotes no authorized execution; an attempted but interrupted operation is UNKNOWN unless independently established FAIL. This is a case-control result, not proof of target enforcement.

For each variant, all authorized attempt histories are retained. A later PASS does not erase an earlier independently established FAIL. Without an independently authenticated correction explaining why earlier evidence was invalid, a verified FAIL remains FAIL. Missing, contradictory, invalidated, retracted or suppressed evidence produces UNKNOWN until resolved; no silent replacement. More than one authorized attempt is not a duplicate if separately identified and bound; duplicate/conflicting records within an attempt force UNKNOWN. Exact authority and correction criteria remain unqualified.

Aggregation is ordered: (1) no attempts for any required variant: case NOT_RUN; (2) any unresolved/contradictory/invalidated or missing required evidence, including a mix of PASS and NOT_RUN: case UNKNOWN; (3) otherwise any independently confirmed FAIL: case FAIL; (4) otherwise all required variants PASS: case PASS. A negative-control PASS counts as that variant's PASS only and never as target qualification. Campaign `INCOMPLETE` has precedence if any required case is UNKNOWN/NOT_RUN or required acceptance, cleanup, seal or binding is unknown; otherwise any case FAIL yields `FAILED`; only all case PASS plus independent reconciliation and valid attestations can yield `LAB_CASES_COMPLETE`. These are bounded lab classifications, not owner acceptance, target authorization or G0 closure. No scoring takes effect until an approved catalog defines every required condition and attestation.

## 3. Oracles and no-transmission evidence

Before any future run the catalog must specify for each variant: fixture and generation binding, independent expected observation, exact approved denial/error classification, positive and negative controls, witness coverage, result mapping, cleanup and redaction. An observation of a generic stop is insufficient. R02 is a synthetic-lab-only READ WRITE mode control, using no write in that variant; independent oracle must prove the setup and detect incorrect acceptance. Any synthetic write control such as R05 is separately authorized, lab-only, and forbidden on target.

R08 requires a separately qualified boundary witness capable of observing every synthetic identity-statement transmission attempt via approved non-sensitive statement IDs, not SQL or personal identity, with independent coverage and positive controls demonstrating that transmissions are detectable. No observed transmission without established complete witness coverage is UNKNOWN, not absence. Race, dropped event, lost witness, replay or mismatched connection stops and yields UNKNOWN. Witness method, authority, transport boundary and safe positive evidence are currently undefined and unqualified; no claim of feasibility follows.

## 4. Version decision precedence — design only

Use exact ASCII bytes with no normalization. Ordered classification: (1) reject as UNKNOWN if non-ASCII, leading/trailing whitespace/control, malformed grammar, missing components, ambiguous syntax or extra numeric segment in the major.minor.patch core; do not record input or a hash; (2) for structurally valid version with a numeric triple differing from `10.11.14`, classify NUMERIC_MISMATCH and stop; (3) for numeric `10.11.14`, only exact full reference `10.11.14-MariaDB-0ubuntu0.24.04.1` yields MATCH_REFERENCE, subject to other gates; (4) any bare, changed or unrecognized suffix is UNKNOWN and stops. Grammar, parser and edge examples require independent source review and qualification; if valid syntax cannot be determined, UNKNOWN. This rule does not modify the accepted owner reference or baseline and does not establish current target version.

## 5. Record lifecycle, redaction and catalog control

Per attempt, proposed immutable records comprise: bound outcome, independently authenticated observer/witness evidence, independently authenticated cleanup/seal disposition, and any independently authenticated invalidation/correction. A separate independently authenticated campaign reconciliation and custody acceptance record is required. Neither the producer nor custodian may self-accept custody evidence; summary and witness attestations must be independently qualified and bound. Role assignment, trust root, anti-replay binding, protected reference, evidence destination and safe identifiers remain unqualified; separate files alone do not prove independence. AI review is not independent human custody.

An invalidation marks a specified attempt or attestation unreliable; a retraction identifies a wrong record. Each requires an independently authenticated append-only disposition with scope, reason class and affected bindings; never overwrite original. On discovery, immediately suspend reliance on affected outcomes and campaign conclusions, including prior LAB_CASES_COMPLETE, until independently reviewed reconciliation. If no safe disposition can be recorded, suppress and treat affected campaign INCOMPLETE through a separately approved safe channel; no raw evidence exception. Retrying or re-basing requires separately authorized scope and preserves failures and limitations.

Fixed canonical catalog order is a design property, not observation or chronological order. Record and manifest must use a separately reviewed fixed safe schema, cardinality and opaque references that do not leak sensitive values through counts, ordering, timing, IDs, hashes or omissions. Suppress unsafe output; inability to reconcile safely means INCOMPLETE, never acceptance. Never hash secrets, unexpected values, canaries or low-entropy confidential values. A synthetic canary escape invalidates the affected attempt and dependent conclusions, stops further work and requires safe incident handling under separately approved scope; no canary, raw output or hash is retained in evidence. Qualification of detection and containment remains open.

A future catalog must be versioned, independently reviewed and frozen before authorization, bound by a reviewed non-secret artifact fingerprint to every attempt and attestation. A change creates a new revision, requires separately current authorization and a documented independently reviewed impact/reconciliation decision; do not automatically carry prior results or claim all changes always invalidate them. Fixed identifiers and private-to-approved-party reconciliation mapping require redaction review. The catalog is not yet frozen, operational, or qualified.
