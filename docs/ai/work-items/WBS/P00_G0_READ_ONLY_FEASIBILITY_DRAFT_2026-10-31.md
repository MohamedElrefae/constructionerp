# P00/G0 — active READ ONLY verification feasibility

Status: source-and-document analysis only; **BLOCKED, not qualified, not an execution grant**. Reference: owner-approved historical baseline MariaDB numeric `10.11.14`, full `10.11.14-MariaDB-0ubuntu0.24.04.1`; neither is a verified current target fact. Changed or unrecognized suffix is UNKNOWN. No target, socket, credentials, custody path, environment configuration, tests or qualification were accessed or performed. The reported unauthorized inspection remains unverified and excluded from evidence, including test fixtures and expected results.

## Decision and evidence boundary

No reviewed source establishes a presently available non-mutating method that demonstrates database-enforced READ ONLY for the particular active target transaction with reliable same-transaction correlation and sufficient visibility. A verifier might be feasible, but this analysis does not prove feasibility or impossibility. An identity query must not pass its gate until the qualified method demonstrates the required property on the same transaction. Lab results, declared mode, metadata alone, successful queries, session defaults, `@@in_transaction`, and absence of a metadata row do not individually satisfy this requirement. No exception or weaker claim is approved.

## Documented leads and limits

1. MariaDB documents a transaction-level READ ONLY access mode and its restrictions, including distinctions from global read-only settings. This documents intended semantics; it does not establish the mode or enforcement of a particular active transaction, nor the exact deployed Ubuntu build. A second START TRANSACTION can implicitly end the previous transaction, so continuation after reconnect, implicit commit, or transaction replacement cannot be presumed. Source: [START TRANSACTION](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/reference/sql-statements/transactions/start-transaction).
2. MariaDB documents `INNODB_TRX`, including the InnoDB transaction read-only indicator, transaction ID, start time and MySQL thread ID. It also warns that its transaction and process-list information can use different snapshots; XA transactions are not represented. The field `TRX_QUERY` may contain statement text and must not be collected. A flagged row is an InnoDB metadata observation, not independent proof of active enforcement across the required operation. Row presence, identity lifetime, freshness, privilege filtering, transaction creation timing and coverage of other engines must be established for the exact candidate. Zero rows means UNKNOWN, not a negative or proof of absence. Source: [INNODB_TRX](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/reference/system-tables/information-schema/information-schema-tables/information-schema-innodb-tables/information-schema-innodb_trx-table).
3. `tx_read_only` concerns the default access mode, and `in_transaction` reports transaction state; neither proves the active transaction's enforced mode. Source: [MariaDB server system variables](https://mariadb.com/docs/server/server-management/variables-and-modes/server-system-variables).
4. MariaDB documents process visibility separately, but that does not establish `INNODB_TRX` visibility for the eventual principal. No PROCESS privilege is assumed or requested. Source: [GRANT / PROCESS](https://github.com/mariadb-corporation/mariadb-docs/blob/main/server/reference/sql-statements/account-management-sql-statements/grant.md).

These public references are general documentation, not pinned proof of the exact historical package, runtime configuration, principal, visibility, or server behavior. They supply design leads, not target evidence.

## Candidate paths and feasibility gates

**A. Existing metadata indicator.** Investigate whether exact-build source establishes that a permitted, non-mutating observation is a complete and current attestation of enforced transaction access mode, rather than only a flag. It must cover the operation and relevant engines, show why restricted visibility cannot create a false pass, and remain bound to the same uninterrupted transaction. The current documentation does not establish these properties; metadata alone currently fails the accepted enforcement-proof requirement. If no such proof exists, reject this candidate as insufficient; do not promote its indicator to proof.

**B. Server-bound non-mutating attestation.** As a design hypothesis only, an independently qualified trusted mechanism could attest active enforcement and connection/transaction identity, bound to an exact reviewed build, server instance and principal. No such mechanism, source, deployment, privilege, trust anchor or independence is established. A mechanism needing installation, modifications, elevated access or production-side changes has no authorization and may be infeasible under existing boundaries. Its qualification cannot be inferred from a lab or from its own self-report. If it cannot provide qualified non-mutating active proof, the gate remains BLOCKED.

**C. Destructive challenge.** A write attempt on the target is outside the accepted non-mutating requirement and is not an alternative. A paired write/read-write control can be specified for a separately authorized disposable qualification environment only; it cannot itself prove target enforcement. It must distinguish permanent and temporary objects and privilege or global-read-only rejection, under a separately reviewed fixture and oracle. No such environment or testing is authorized here.

## Correlation, visibility, and stop requirements

- Specify and independently substantiate a server-origin transaction/session binding, freshness, connection identity reuse, races with other sessions of the same principal, and transaction continuity. An identifier or timestamp alone cannot establish uninterrupted continuity; guard against pooling, reconnect, implicit commit, replacement and mixed snapshots. Where any binding or continuity is uncertain, stop before identity queries and classify INCOMPLETE/UNKNOWN.
- Specify exact, least-privilege principal and allowed fields; establish visibility and result completeness for the exact observation using independent evidence. A successful query is not visibility evidence. Unexpected schema, filtered/multiple/stale rows, unverified engine coverage or inaccessible required metadata stops. Never retry with broader privileges after a stop.
- For any later qualification design, require positive and negative independent oracles covering writable mode, same-principal competing sessions, stale or reused identifiers, absent/filtered rows, transaction replacement, implicit commit, disconnect, and pre-gate identity-query non-transmission. Require a matched read-write positive control to distinguish transaction enforcement from unrelated write rejection. Record case outcomes without suppressed values, credentials, raw SQL/script bodies, users, document identifiers, or unrelated business data.
- Define the allowable transaction lifetime, safe logging and redaction, qualified rollback and connection closure. Failure to attest rollback or closure leaves the attempt incomplete; do not automatically retry. Stop records are fixed/coarsened and withheld if even a generic status could disclose a suppressed value. Confirmed adverse findings must remain visible in a safely protected record; if safe preservation or independent attestation is unavailable, overall status remains incomplete.

## Required evidence, not yet available

2. A documented non-mutating active enforcement proof and independent assurance that its observation cannot pass on a writable, stale, replaced, different or inaccessible transaction; exact uncertainty handling.
3. Independent evidence for session/transaction correlation, visibility and permissions, as well as observer independence, non-transmission before the gate, redaction, logging, cleanup and protected evidence integrity. Specify reliable negative controls and their independent oracles before any qualification request.
4. Separately authorized, independently reviewed qualification design and environment, if feasible; later target binding and per-attempt evidence cannot be replaced by lab results. No target or environment operation is authorized by this paper.
5. Custody and acceptance arrangements consistent with the existing governance: no independent human adjudicator participates; the custodian cannot self-accept custody evidence; AI review is not independent human custody. The compatible process remains unresolved.
