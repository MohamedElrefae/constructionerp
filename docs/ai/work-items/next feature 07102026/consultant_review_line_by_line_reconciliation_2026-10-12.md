## 4. Certificate design to carry forward

Use our engineering report as the design baseline, incorporating useful report/register ideas from the external review. Before implementation, agree the following:

1. **Identity and contract terms:** company, contract, party, currency and series; versioned terms and approved opening balances. A Frappe Single DocType is a singleton, not a collection with one record per BOQ. Use an ordinary linked/versioned record if each contract needs its own instance. [W2]
2. **Source evidence:** dated measurements and explicit multi-stage allocations. Calculate available quantity under lock; drafts do not establish final entitlement. Include opening consumption and corrections.
3. **Valuation:** preserve prior approved amounts; snapshot applicable rate, factor, UOM and revision. Apply factor once on the agreed nominal/physical quantity basis. Do not sum historical cumulative columns or reprice all historical work.
4. **Deductions and release:** distinguish retention withheld, held and released; recover against evidenced actual advances, accounting for pending allocations. Material-on-site valuation is not automatically a deduction and remains outside the first measured-work pilot unless specified.
5. **Lifecycle:** separate internal workflow, native docstatus, customer certification and derived invoice status. Define what resets verification and how customer rejection/correction is represented.
6. **Invoice integration:** idempotently generate a draft, retain BOQ item/source traceability, then let Finance review/post. Grouped presentation is acceptable only if durable detailed accounting allocations preserve existing controls. No direct automatic posting policy is approved by this review.
7. **Reversal:** consider downstream invoices, payments, later certificates, releases, recoveries and closed periods. Use eligible reversal or approved linked corrections; never erase approval history.
8. **Qualification:** independent worked examples, allowed/denied users, actual browser/print tests, concurrent allocation, retries, injected failures, migrations and two operational billing cycles. Accounting acceptance requires a gross/tax/deductions/net/ledger reconciliation, not a vague equality of every GL total to certificate net.

Finance still must confirm the actual tax and posting treatment. This review does not prescribe Egyptian tax rates, statutory invoice bases or mandatory account numbers. The applicable integration route is a delivery requirement, and the connector can be maintained separately from the certificate module. [M1], [M2]
